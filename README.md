## 🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to **greenprompt**! This application helps you analyze, optimize, and track the energy usage, cost, and carbon footprint of your large language model (LLM) prompts. With research-backed recommendations and real-time dashboards, you can make smarter, more sustainable decisions.

## 🖥️ System Requirements

Before you start, ensure your system meets the following requirements:

- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or later, macOS Mojave or later, or a recent version of Linux (Ubuntu 20.04 or later recommended).
- **Memory (RAM):** At least 4 GB required; 8 GB or more recommended.
- **Disk Space:** At least 100 MB of free space.
- **Internet Access:** A stable internet connection for software updates and data retrieval.

## 📊 Features

- **Energy Analysis:** Understand the energy consumption of your LLM prompts with easy-to-read visuals.
- **Cost Tracking:** See how much you spend on energy for your models and prompts.
- **Carbon Footprint Calculation:** Evaluate the environmental impact of your LLM usage.
- **Real-time Dashboards:** Access live updates on energy use and costs directly within the app.
- **Recommendations:** Get research-backed suggestions to lower costs and improve efficiency in your prompt usage.
